01About

I'm a backend-leaning full-stack engineer based in Dhaka, currently a Senior Software Engineer at Enosis Solutions, where I work on engineering-simulation data platforms, similarity search over legal case data, and LLM-assisted test authoring — mostly in Java, Spring Boot, TypeScript and Python.

Before that, at REVE Systems, I built and led delivery on government-scale systems — office management tools for Bangladesh's Parliament, a bond-license automation platform for 10,000+ users, and a notification system moving roughly a million messages a day.

I grew up moving around Bangladesh — Chittagong, Dhaka, Jashore — following my father's postings, before studying Computer Science and Engineering at BUET. That early exposure to different environments still shows up in how I work: comfortable across unfamiliar codebases, teams, and problem domains.

Outside of production work, I'm a long-time competitive-math and olympiad person — a founding member of Trivuj Math Club and an academic member of the Bangladesh Mathematical Olympiad Committee — and I teach, having recorded 70+ hours of Bangla-language CS and math lectures on YouTube.
